A Digital Jukebox for Your Home, Game Room and Parties

JukeANator turns your own music collection into a classic, bar-style jukebox. It has the look and feel of a CD jukebox, with the search, browsing and playlist power of a modern digital jukebox, so it is just as much at home in a game room cabinet as it is on a desktop computer or in a custom touchscreen jukebox you build yourself.

 

CD Jukebox Look and Feel

Its screens were designed to mimic the simplicity of CD jukeboxes, yet offer ways to search for music, browse by popularity or browse by genre. It is touchscreen friendly, so family and guests can pick songs without a keyboard or mouse.

Great for Parties and DJs

The powerful administration screen lets you DJ a party with ease: queue up many songs at once, load and save playlists, move songs up or down the queue, interlace sound clips between songs and see the total running time of everything queued. Leave the cases of easily damaged CDs at home.

Build Your Own Jukebox

There are basically 3 ways to get a JukeANator jukebox into your home or game room:
  • Purchase a ready-to-install JukeANator digital jukebox (as pictured above)
  • Purchase a JukeANator jukebox conversion kit and retrofit your existing CD jukebox yourself
  • Purchase just the JukeANator software and build/retrofit your own jukebox using your own computer and touchscreen, since JukeANator uses 100% "off-the-shelf" components

Song Library Tools

JukeANator keeps your song library accurate and easy to manage:
  1. Automatic copyright information retrieval from Amazon.com
  2. Automatic song ISRC retrieval from MusicBrainz
  3. Enhanced song metadata extraction for proper Amazon/MusicBrainz automatic web service lookups
  4. CD/Song properties editor for manual editing of publisher/copyright info, artist/song names and song ISRC

The key to the ease-of-use is how music is organized, chosen and played. Music is categorized by genre, artist and CD title, and an automatic numbering scheme gives each song in the library a unique 4 or 5 digit number (depending upon how many CD titles there are). Songs can be chosen either by their unique number, or by touching or clicking on them. Chosen songs are added to the "Song Queue", which can be adjusted on the fly: add or remove songs and change the order in which they will be played.
