Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Digital Jukebox Technologies, LLC ("DJT", "we") collects, uses and protects personal information when you use the www.jukeanator.com website, the JukeANator Web/Mobile app and JukeANator jukeboxes.

Information We Collect
  • Contact and order information: name, email address, mailing address and phone number that you provide when you place an order or use the contact form.
  • Account information (Web/Mobile app): first name, last name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form.
  • App activity: your credit balance, credit purchases, the songs you queue, your play and search history and your playlists, so the app can show them back to you.
  • Location: at jukeboxes that accept song requests only from people on the premises, the app asks your device for its location when you queue a song and sends it with that request so the jukebox can confirm that you are nearby. Your location is checked and is not stored. If you deny location access, you will not be able to queue songs at those jukeboxes.
  • Technical information: as with most websites, our servers record information such as your IP address, browser type and operating system.
How We Use Information

We use this information to fulfill your order and send you an order confirmation, to create and operate your account, to verify your email address, to add purchased credits to your account and play the songs you request, to respond to customer service requests, and to keep our services secure. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Payments

Payments are processed by PayPal, including Braintree (a PayPal service) for credit and debit card, PayPal, Venmo, Google Pay and Apple Pay payments in the Web/Mobile app. You provide your payment details directly to the payment processor; DJT does not receive or store your full card number. The payment processor's privacy policy applies to the information you provide to it.

Cookies and Local Storage

The Web/Mobile app stores your sign-in token, email address and recent searches on your device so that you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Access, Correction and Deletion

You can view and update your account information in the Web/Mobile app under Manage Account, and you can delete your account there with Delete Account. For any other request, or for questions about this policy, email support (at) jukeanator.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
